# Theo — Printing Guide (v1 prototype, glue-together)

8 parts: Body (teal), FacePlate (white), EarL/R (dark teal), Eyes+Mouth (black), Bell (gold), BowTie (gold).
Print plate/eyes/mouth/bowtie flat-back down; bell dome-up (small support under peg, or snip peg and glue).
Parts nest into shallow recesses on the body - 0.15 mm tolerance, glue to fix.
NOTE: superseded design - the snap-fit teardrop version is planned to replace this kit.
