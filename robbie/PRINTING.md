# Robbie — Printing Guide

**Parts:** FrontShell + BackShell (orange), FaceBlock (white), UiCard screen topper (white), Base (any).
Shells seam-face down (supports on interior ceiling cap only); FaceBlock flat back down;
UiCard flat on its back; Base flat. "Ui" is recessed on the card as a paint/Sharpie guide.
**Assembly:** FaceBlock into window -> UiCard tab into crown slot (clamped at close) ->
snap BackShell -> tail into Base dish. 0.25 mm tolerances.
